# Moringa Boost Feature - COMPLETE 🌿

## What Was Added

### 1. "Boost with Moringa" Toggle
- **Where**: Inside "View Nutritional Info" modal
- **What**: Interactive toggle switch to add/remove 1 tsp of Moringa Powder
- **Visuals**: Beautiful green styling with "✨ Miracle Product" branding

### 2. Live Dynamic Updates
- Toggling the switch **instantly recalculates** all nutrition numbers:
  - Energy (kcal/kJ)
  - Protein
  - Carbs
  - All Vitamins & Minerals

### 3. "See The Difference" Visualization
- When boosted, key nutrients show a **Green Badge** with the exact increase:
  - `+112µg` Vitamin A
  - `+40mg` Calcium
  - `+0.8mg` Iron
  - `+0.4mg` Riboflavin
- Makes the nutritional power of Moringa **undeniable** at a glance.

## How It Works

1. **Auto-Detection**: 
   - When you open a recipe like "Kale & Egg Power Smoothie" (which has Moringa), the toggle starts **ON**.
   - For other recipes (e.g., standard meal plan items), it starts **OFF**.

2. **Base Calculation**:
   - The system calculates the recipe's "Base" nutrition (stripping out existing Moringa if present).
   - This ensures we don't double-count when you toggle it ON.

3. **Standard Dose**:
   - The toggle adds exactly **1 tsp (2g)** of Moringa Powder.
   - Using the precise Cronometer data we seeded (250 kcal/100g, etc.).

## Try It Out

1. Generate a meal plan.
2. Open **"View Nutritional Info"** for any recipe.
3. Click the **Boost with Moringa** toggle.
4. Watch the numbers jump up! 
5. Look for the green `+` badges next to Vitamin A, B2, Iron, etc.

## Why This Matters
It fulfills the key goal: **"Show the power of moringa powder"**. Users can literally toggle it on/off to see how empty their meal is without it, and how nutrient-dense it becomes WITH it.
